About Us

The story behind Protoplast.3D

Our Story – Protoplast.3D

It all started with a simple question: Why should innovation be limited by access to manufacturing?

We saw engineers struggling to bring their ideas to life, startups delaying product launches due to high prototyping costs, and businesses dependent on slow, expensive manufacturing methods. That's when Protoplast.3D was born—out of a need to bridge the gap between creativity and production.

What began as a small initiative has grown into a cutting-edge digital manufacturing hub. From 3D printing to CNC cutting, PCB designing to CAD modeling, we've built a space where ideas take shape, products evolve, and businesses scale faster than ever before.

Today, we work with engineers, designers, students, and industry leaders, offering them the tools to create without limits. Whether it's a custom prototype, precision part, or electronic circuit board, we ensure every project is handled with expertise, speed, and precision.

At Protoplast.3D, we believe the future isn't something you wait for—it's something you create. And we're here to help you build it.

3D Printing

At Protoplast.3D, we specialize in high-precision 3D printing services, offering FDM and SLA printing technologies to cater to a wide range of industries. Whether you need functional prototypes, custom components, architectural models, or production-grade parts, our advanced printing techniques ensure exceptional quality and accuracy.

Materials Available:

  • PLA, ABS, PETG – Ideal for strong, lightweight, and functional parts.
  • Resin Printing (SLA/DLP) – Perfect for highly detailed, smooth-finish components.

Applications:

  • Product Prototyping – Validate designs before mass production.
  • Automotive & Aerospace – Lightweight and strong components for specialized applications.
  • Medical & Healthcare – Custom prosthetics, surgical guides, and medical models.
  • Consumer Goods – Customized accessories, tools, and home decor.

CNC Cutting & Machining

Our CNC cutting and machining services provide high-precision fabrication for various materials, including metals, plastics, acrylics, and wood. Using advanced CNC machines, we guarantee clean cuts, fine detailing, and excellent repeatability, making it ideal for custom components, signage, and industrial applications.

Capabilities:

  • 2D & 3D CNC Machining – For custom industrial and mechanical parts.
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving – For detailed markings and precision cutting.
  • Milling & Routing – High-accuracy shaping for metal, wood, and acrylic components.

Applications:

  • Industrial & Mechanical Parts – Customized and batch-manufactured machine parts.
  • Architectural & Interior Designs – CNC-cut panels, engravings, and decorative elements.
  • Signage & Branding – Custom-designed acrylic and metal signboards.
